# Keyword research exam (KW)

> What is our audience actually typing, and is it worth a page?

### KW-01 Understand the audience
**Question:** What problem is the searcher trying to solve?
**Test:** From the site, the codebase's marketing copy and the user, write one sentence: who searches, what problem they have, what they do next.
**Healthy:** The user agrees with the sentence.
**If you can't test:** Ask the user this one question before continuing.

### KW-02 Find seed keywords
**Question:** What would a customer call this if they had never heard our jargon?
**Test:** List 10–20 seeds in problem language, not feature names. Best source: real queries in Search Console. Next best: support tickets, sales calls, forum threads.
**Healthy:** Most seeds are words a customer would type, not words from our navigation.
**If you can't test:** Draft seeds from the site and label them unvalidated.

### KW-03 Use one keyword tool
**Question:** Which one tool are we using?
**Test:** Check for a connected tool (Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking, DataForSEO, etc.). Pick one and use it for every metric in this audit.
**Healthy:** Every number in the report names the same source.
**If you can't test:** Mark volume and difficulty Untested. Never estimate them. One keyword once read KD 82 in one tool and KD 0 in another.

### KW-04 Analyse search intent
**Question:** Informational, navigational, commercial or transactional, and does our page match?
**Test:** If web search is available, look at what ranks now: page type (guide, list, tool, product, comparison), angle and freshness. Compare with our page.
**Healthy:** Our page is the same kind of thing that ranks, with a better answer.
**If you can't test:** Infer intent from the query's wording and say it is inferred.

### KW-05 Check the metrics
**Question:** Volume, difficulty, CPC, trend. Which of these actually changes our decision?
**Test:** Pull only the metric that would change the decision. A niche term with 30 searches and strong buying intent can beat a 5,000-search term with none.
**Healthy:** Each target keyword has a stated decision and the one metric behind it.

### KW-06 Select and organise
**Question:** Does this keyword deserve its own page, or is it a section of one?
**Test:** Group keywords one page would answer (same intent, overlapping results). One group, one page. Check for cannibalisation: two of our pages targeting one group, or one query alternating between URLs in Search Console.
**Healthy:** A keyword-to-page map where no two pages compete.
