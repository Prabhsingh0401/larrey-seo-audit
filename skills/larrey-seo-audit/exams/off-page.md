# Off-page exam (OF)

> Who else says we exist?

### OF-01 Backlinks
**Question:** Would this link send us a customer even if search engines did not exist?
**Test:** With a backlink tool: referring domains, their relevance, recently lost links, and links pointing to our 404s (reclaim them with redirects).
**Healthy:** Links from places our buyers read.
**If you can't test:** Untested. Never guess domain authority.

### OF-02 Guest posts
**Question:** Are we publishing where our buyers read, or where links are cheap?
**Test:** List target publications and check each one has our audience. Flag link farms and paid-link schemes.
**Healthy:** A short list of publications a customer would actually read.

### OF-03 Brand mentions
**Question:** Are we counting mentions as well as links?
**Test:** Search the brand name, excluding our own domain. Find unlinked mentions, and "best X for Y" lists and comparisons in our category that leave us out. These are the pages AI assistants cite.
**Healthy:** We appear in the lists and comparisons for our category.

### OF-04 Social
**Question:** Social signals are not a ranking factor. Who is seeing us there?
**Test:** Are we active where the audience actually is? Does each post give a reason to click?
**Healthy:** Social is treated as distribution and brand, not as ranking.

### OF-05 Local listings
**Question:** Are the name, address, phone and hours identical everywhere they appear?
**Test:** Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places and the main directories for the country and industry. Only for businesses with a location or service area; skip otherwise.
**Healthy:** Identical details everywhere.

### OF-06 Community
**Question:** Who recommends us when we are not in the room?
**Test:** Search Reddit, forums, GitHub, Stack Overflow, YouTube and review sites for the category question. Are we mentioned? Is what's said accurate?
**Healthy:** Real customers mention us. Never suggest fake accounts or planted reviews: they break platform rules, and Google warns against manufactured mentions.
**Why it matters:** Vendor research suggests most AI brand mentions come from third-party pages (see sources; the vendor sells an off-site product, so treat the exact figure with care).
