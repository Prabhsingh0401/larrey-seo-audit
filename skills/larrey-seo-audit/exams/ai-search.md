# AI search exam (AI)

> Are we found and recommended by assistants?

### AI-01 Is AI taking our traffic?
**Question:** How much traffic comes from AI assistants, compared with organic search?
**Test:** In GA4, find referral sessions from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com. Compare their volume and growth with organic.
**Healthy:** Both are measured. In Shopify's Q2 2026 data, AI-referred sessions grew far faster than organic, but organic still sent more traffic than all AI platforms combined. Both grew, so don't abandon search.

### AI-02 Do we need AI markup?
**Question:** Do we need llms.txt, AI text files or special schema?
**Test:** Note any llms.txt or "AI schema" on the site: harmless, but not a fix. Confirm key pages are indexed and eligible to show with a snippet (no `nosnippet` on pages that should be cited).
**Healthy:** Effort goes to crawlability and content. Google's generative AI guide says AI Overviews and AI Mode build on core Search systems and need no special files, Markdown versions or AI-only schema.

### AI-03 Where does AI read?
**Question:** Is the answer in the first third of the page?
**Test:** Check that the key claim, numbers and definitions appear in the first 30% of the page. Check that important sentences still make sense alone, without "this" or "as mentioned above".
**Healthy:** Front-loaded answers and self-contained sentences. Research found that a large share of ChatGPT citations come from the first 30% of a page (see sources).

### AI-04 How long should a page be?
**Question:** Does each section answer one question on its own, without the rest of the page?
**Test:** Read each H2 section in isolation.
**Healthy:** Every section stands alone. Focused pages beat sprawling "ultimate guides" for ChatGPT citations (see sources).

### AI-05 Used more than once?
**Question:** Are we answering many questions, or chasing one?
**Test:** List the follow-up questions around the topic (from search, forums and "People also ask"). Count how many the site answers.
**Healthy:** The site answers the whole cluster, so it can be cited across many prompts, not one.

### AI-06 How do we measure it?
**Question:** Which platform moved?
**Test:** Run the same fixed prompt set on each platform (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude) on a schedule. Record which pages and brands are cited. Answers vary between runs, so repeat each prompt.
**Healthy:** Visibility is tracked per platform, not as one blended score. Most AI citations appear in only one engine (see sources).
